# Qt / PySide6 / Shiboken

This portable application includes unmodified, dynamically linked Qt 6.12.0,
PySide6 6.12.0 and Shiboken6 6.12.0 libraries. The open-source LGPLv3 option
applies to these included libraries. The Qt libraries reside under _internal
and may be replaced with compatible builds. Library-related reverse engineering
for debugging modifications is permitted under LGPLv3.

License texts: LGPL-3.0.txt and GPL-3.0.txt in this folder; package notices
are also preserved under the neighboring license folders.

Upstream source repositories and exact release:

- Qt 6.12.0: https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtbase.git/?h=v6.12.0
- PySide6 and Shiboken6 6.12.0: https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/?h=v6.12.0
- Source download index: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/

The assistant source is provided separately in the project source ZIP.
Pikafish retains its GPLv3 license and corresponding source in engine/.
